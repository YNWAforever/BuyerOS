# Prepare a proposal
Consume ProposalArguments with buyer/profile, stated pain_points and one to three supplied CommercialTerms. Each term contains Decimal price/currency, scope, exclusions, timeline and evidence. Ask for missing verified commercial inputs; never fabricate a price tier, case study, promised ROI or customer approval.

DB-only preparation additionally requires explicit case_study_refs from the selected current sources. It quotes their evidence without inventing customer success claims. Missing price/terms or case-study proof is Needs input. Grounded authored model sections remain available through the separately admitted model path.

Lead with the customer's stated problem, then the proposed solution and explicit deliverables. Carry the supplied terms without inventing discounts or payment terms. Explain assumptions and uncertainties. Use only verified proof points; projected benefits are clearly conditional and must not become guarantees. Concision and honest exclusions take priority over a fixed page count.

Return sales_proposal with problem, solution, terms, summary, unknowns, assumptions, proof_points and optional followup preparation. Every factual commercial claim links to selected evidence. Keep draft and approval state distinct. Optional follow-up messages are suggestions, not scheduled events.

documents.prepare produces a versioned document through native ownership. The skill cannot sign, approve, send or replace a live commercial agreement. Check exact native/dependency revisions before save; if the underlying proposal/draft changes, retain the proposed result and require review again. Completion is persisted/readback proposal output with review_required true.
