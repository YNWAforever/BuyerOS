### Features

- **Buyer criteria and versions:** Keep a record as requirements change. Organize your offer, target markets and ideal buyer criteria. Retain versions as requirements change so your team can revisit them.
- **Company records and sources:** Keep information alongside its provenance. Inspect company information and source records to understand the basis for a decision. A source does not guarantee accuracy or freshness.
- **Review and assignment:** Show the current review status and owner. Use review status and assignment to support handovers. Access to product data is scoped by workspace and membership.
- **Outreach drafts and revisions:** Save first, review next, and retain revisions. Build a fixed template from approved information and sources, then edit and review it with a colleague. This workflow does not offer sending.
- **Import an existing list:** CSV and XLSX are supported; imported claims still need review. List imports have format and row limits. Importing a record does not verify its company information or sources.
- **Work with offer documents:** Use PDF, PPTX, TXT and MD documents. Organize offer context for your buyer criteria. Document handling has format and file-size limits and does not support every file type.

### Workflow

- **Define the criteria:** Describe your offer, target markets and ideal buyers. Organize the team’s requirements and retain criteria versions so changes can be reviewed later.
- **Organize and review:** Inspect company records, sources and review status. Assess the available information and what still needs checking, then assign a colleague. Third-party sources still require human judgment.
- **Save a draft:** Prepare a message from approved information for a colleague to review. Colleagues can edit the fixed template and save revisions. The draft is unsent; this workflow does not offer sending.

### Intended roles

- **Business development:** Organize criteria and next steps so colleagues understand the follow-up context.
- **Researchers:** Keep company records, sources and assessments together.
- **Reviewers:** Inspect the content and its sources before giving feedback.
